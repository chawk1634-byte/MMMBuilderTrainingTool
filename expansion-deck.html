The Business Case

The Expansion Problem

In established markets, ambient familiarity drives trial. In new western markets, every guest must be earned — and not all of them are worth the same cost to acquire.

The right question isn't how many people come in on opening week. It's whether we're acquiring guests who will still be here in month twelve — and whether they're bringing people with them.

Activation Playbook

Every tactic has a measurement attached. We're not running programs — we're running experiments with pre-specified success criteria.

Phase ▪ Urban Enthusiast Recruit ▪ Brand Advocate Retain + Amplify
Pre-Opening Food media + influencer seeding. "Coming soon" content framed as discovery. A new opening is an event — treat it like one. LTO early access + cross-market ambassador seeding. Engage existing advocates in adjacent markets to build West Coast social presence before day one.
Opening Event-style launch designed for shareability. Full-menu trial incentive. KPI: Shakes+Burgers basket share, week 1. UGC program activation + referral mechanic. Surface advocate content in new market feeds. KPI: referral attribution rate.
Post-Visit App download trigger + loyalty onboarding. Order-specific follow-up. KPI: 30-day return rate by first-basket type. NPS-triggered outreach + LTO co-creation invite. Loyalty points for social shares. KPI: NPS trend, LTO participation rate.
Ongoing Monitor Urban Enthusiast share of new visitors monthly. Target ≥25% of new guest mix. Below threshold → adjust acquisition channel. Track cohort conversion to advocate behavior vs. established market benchmark. Below threshold → investigate first-visit experience.

The playbook operationalizes the pipeline. I want to flag the design principle: every tactic has a measurement attached. We're not running programs. The Urban Enthusiast side is about recruitment and conversion — getting them in, giving them the right first experience, building the app relationship. The Brand Advocate side is about amplification — leveraging existing advocates in adjacent markets to seed social presence before we even open, then deepening engagement through LTO access and referral mechanics post-launch.
